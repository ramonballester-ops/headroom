Add a leg

Enter block time (H:MM — the out-off-on-in figure from the leg detail screen). Out-to-in clock time is NOT block time — it contains the timezone shift. Block-in is the arrival moment, never departure: a redeye departing day D blocks in day D+1. Deadheads: check DH — logged, paid, excluded from the cap.
